Lane Seven

An outdoor range with a pistol, a rifle and a pump shotgun laid out on the bench. Pick a gun for each hand, then shoot ROUND for a 60-second score attack, DRILL for the El Presidente par drill, or PRACTICE for relaxed steel and rail shooting.

Checking for a VR headset…

Controls

  • Pick up: squeeze grip near a gun on the bench. Squeeze again to set it down.
  • Two hands: the rifle and shotgun need your other hand on the front grip. Hold grip there to support and aim.
  • Fire: trigger. Reload: A / B / X / Y. The shotgun loads one shell at a time until full.
  • Sights: pistols have three-dot iron sights, the shotgun has beads, the rifle has a red-dot optic.
  • Screen: click fires, R reloads, 1 / 2 / 3 picks pistol, rifle, shotgun, Esc opens this menu.

Modes and scoring

  • Round: 60 s. Paper 1.0–10.9 ×10, steel 50/75, pop-ups up to 125, rail ×1.5. Combo up to ×4.
  • Practice: only the swinging steel and the rail target. No timer; tracks hits, accuracy and streak. Shoot END to stop.
  • Drill: 3 targets at 9 m, 2 shots each, forced reload, 2 more each. Ring points plus 20 per second under the 10 s par.
