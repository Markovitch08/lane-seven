A 60-second round on an indoor pistol range. Paper scores by ring down to the decimal, steel plates ring out at 12 m, pop-ups drop fast, and one target rides the rail at 14 m. Shoot the START board to begin.

Controls

  • VR: trigger fires, A / X or grip reloads
  • Screen: click fires, R or right-click reloads, Esc opens this menu
  • 12 rounds per magazine

Scoring

  • Paper: 1.0 to 10.9 per hit, ×10
  • Steel: 50, or 75 in the center
  • Pop-up: up to 100 for speed, +25 center
  • Rail target: ring score ×1.5
  • Combo ×2 at 3 hits, ×3 at 6, ×4 at 10. A miss resets it.
